Shorts Monitor

Personal analytics for the creator's own TikTok account.

Shorts Monitor connects to the account owner's TikTok profile through Login Kit and reads the owner's public videos through the Display API. It shows views, likes, comments and engagement over time, so the creator can see which videos perform best and plan future content.

No videos are downloaded, republished or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.
