Shorts Monitor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What data we read

Shorts Monitor reads statistics of the TikTok account that authorizes it: the account's public video list, view counts, like counts, comment counts and publication dates.

Where data is stored

Data is stored locally on the user's own computer, in the user's profile folder. No server of ours receives or stores the data.

Tokens

OAuth tokens are stored locally and used only to refresh access to the same account. They are never transmitted to third parties or sold.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer personal data to any third party. We do not use the data for advertising.

Retention

Data is kept until the user deletes the local files or revokes access in TikTok settings.

Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
